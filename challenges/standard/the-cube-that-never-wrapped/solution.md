# Solution: The Cube That Never Wrapped

Category: Crypto
Points: 300

## Flag

`SCSS{small_e_needs_padding}`

## Write-up

Because e=3 and m^3<n, c is the ordinary integer cube of m. Compute the exact integer cube root of c, convert the result to big-endian bytes, and decode UTF-8.
